## 3. What is new today

Three artifacts, ready to hand to Codex as **MP-7**:

- **`ayurveda-data/rules/food-roles.json` rev1** — the missing field. 14 roles, 27 rules, 615 phrases, per-role portion ranges, a `requiresCooking` flag and a near-duplicate headword test. This exists because `minAgeMonths` is a *floor*, not a range: infant formula carries a floor of 0 and therefore satisfies every adult age check in the system. The catalogue could never express "intended for infants, inappropriate for adults", nor "this is a seasoning, not a dish". Both gaps produced the same symptom.
- **`ayurveda-data/tests/food-role-goldens.json` rev1** — 71 role cases (24 transcribed verbatim from the failing sample), 16 cooking-flag cases, 11 duplicate cases, 6 contested. Labelled a **training set**, with a mandatory 60-row random holdout, because MP-3 scored 56/56 on its own corpus and 35/48 on a set written afterwards.
- **`plan-validity-properties.json` rev2** — 46 properties, 30 hard. New `plausibility` group C1–C10, bound to the taxonomy so the ranges live in one place.

**FC-1f, found while validating the above.** `food-concepts.json` rev5 specifies plural tolerance as trailing-`s` only. That does not match `potatoes` from `potato`, `berries` from `berry`, or `leaves` from `leaf`. It surfaced when *"Potatoes, flesh and skin, raw"* — the exact row behind the original `flesh` defect — fell through to `other`. Fix plus a re-run of all 117 exclusion goldens is folded into MP-7.

## 4. The risk I want visible

**Y1 may collapse under MP-7, and that would be a finding, not a regression.**

The tier preference rewards classical-tier dravyas. Classical-tier dravyas are overwhelmingly spices and herbs. MP-7 caps seasonings at 2 per meal. If the +0.5209 pacification delta was substantially produced by the solver loading meals with classical spices, capping them shrinks it — and the honest reading is that some of the measured Ayurvedic effect was an artifact of that bias rather than dosha-appropriate food selection.

The fix in that case is to weight dosha effect by *portion actually consumed* rather than by presence, so 2 g of dried marjoram cannot pacify a constitution as much as 200 g of a suitable main. MP-7 §6 says this explicitly and forbids raising the cap to protect the number.

## 7. Method note

The three practices in rev3 §7 — held-out measurement, cross-derivation, property-based acceptance — all did their jobs and all missed this.

The gap was narrower than any of them: **property-based acceptance only tests the properties you thought to write.** Structure, nutrition, safety, variety, dosha, determinism, feasibility and performance were all asserted. Whether the output was food was not.

The practice worth adding: **read the actual output.** MP-6b's sample was requested for narration copy quality, and the defect was visible in the first meal. Thirty-six properties and 123 tests did not see what one read-through did.
