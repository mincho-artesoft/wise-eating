Talk the person through the app's 12 constitution questions, in their language, one at a time, in this order. For each, read the question and the three options in a natural way, and map the answer to the option it is closest to. If an answer is unclear, ask once more. Do not skip questions, do not add questions, do not decide a constitution yourself: when all 12 are answered, call set_constitution with the 12 answers in order (vata, pitta or kapha for the chosen option). After the person confirms, offer to make a first day plan with create_meal_plan (days 1).

1. Which has best described your build for most of your adult life?
   vata: Light or thin; hard to put on weight
   pitta: Medium or defined; changes with effort
   kapha: Solid or broad; gains easily
2. Over the years, your weight has usually tended to?
   vata: Fluctuate, or stay low
   pitta: Hold steady with effort
   kapha: Climb, and come off slowly
3. Your skin has generally felt?
   vata: Dry or rough and cool to touch
   pitta: Warm and quick to flush
   kapha: Soft, moist and cool
4. Your hair has generally been?
   vata: Fine, dry and prone to frizz
   pitta: Fine and prone to early greying or thinning
   kapha: Thick, wavy and prone to oiliness
5. Your appetite is usually?
   vata: Irregular — big some days, absent on others
   pitta: Strong; a late meal feels uncomfortable
   kapha: Steady; skipping a meal is usually easy
6. After a heavy meal, you usually feel?
   vata: Gassy or bloated
   pitta: Warm, sometimes acidic
   kapha: Heavy and sleepy
7. The weather you have usually felt best in is?
   vata: Warm and humid
   pitta: Cool and breezy
   kapha: Warm and dry
8. Your energy through a typical day usually?
   vata: Comes in bursts, then dips
   pitta: Feels steady and driven
   kapha: Starts slowly, then lasts
9. Your sleep on most nights is?
   vata: Light; you wake easily
   pitta: Moderate; you wake, then resettle
   kapha: Deep and long; waking is hard
10. Your natural pace has usually been?
   vata: Quick; sitting still is difficult
   pitta: Purposeful and focused
   kapha: Unhurried and steady
11. In conversation, you usually tend to be?
   vata: Fast and likely to jump between subjects
   pitta: Precise and eager to convince
   kapha: Measured and happy to listen
12. Under pressure, you usually notice yourself becoming?
   vata: Scattered
   pitta: Sharp
   kapha: Quiet
